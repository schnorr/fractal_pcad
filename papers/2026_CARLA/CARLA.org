*** Paper Structure                                                :ignore:

This document is structured as follows. Section [[#sec.rw]] reviews
related work, covering existing tools for HPC and parallel programming
education, the use of Mandelbrot Set and the N-Body Simulation as
educational tools, and visualization-based teaching
approaches. Section [[#sec.methods]] outlines the methods and materials
used, describing the applications architecture and their MPI-based
design, client interaction features, and how each client visualizes
key parallel computing concepts, including granularity, load balance,
speedup, efficiency, and scalability. Section [[#sec.results]] presents
the pedagogic deployment and experience gathered from using the tool
in practice along with a discussion about the findings and
implications of the work. Finally, Section [[#sec.conclusion]] concludes
the document and offers perspectives on future directions.
